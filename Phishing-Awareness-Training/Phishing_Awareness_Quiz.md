# Phishing Awareness Quiz

**Instructions:** Select the best answer for each question. Passing score: 8/10.

## Questions

1. An email says your account will close in 10 minutes. What should you do?

   * A. Click its link immediately
   * B. Verify through the official app or a trusted channel
   * C. Reply with your password
2. What does HTTPS guarantee?

   * A. The website is legitimate
   * B. The connection is encrypted, but the website could still be fraudulent
   * C. Every download is safe
3. Your manager emails an urgent request to transfer money to a new account. What is safest?

   * A. Transfer immediately
   * B. Verify through a separate trusted channel
   * C. Ignore all payment procedures
4. Which is a phishing warning sign?

   * A. An unexpected request for your verification code
   * B. A website opened using your trusted bookmark
   * C. A routine task you expected
5. What is spear phishing?

   * A. A targeted phishing attempt
   * B. A firewall configuration
   * C. A software update
6. You clicked a suspicious link but entered no information. What should you do?

   * A. Hide the incident
   * B. Close the page and report it according to policy
   * C. Forward the link to everyone
7. You entered your password on a suspicious website. What should you do?

   * A. Change the affected password using a trusted device and contact the relevant security team
   * B. Wait several days
   * C. Email your password to IT
8. Why can an email display name be misleading?

   * A. It always proves the sender's identity
   * B. Attackers can imitate trusted names
   * C. It cannot be changed
9. How should you access an account after receiving a suspicious link?

   * A. Use the link
   * B. Open the official app or a known trusted address
   * C. Choose the first advertisement in search results
10. What should you do with a suspicious work email?

    * A. Forward it to all colleagues
    * B. Download its attachment
    * C. Use the approved phishing-reporting channel

## Answer Key



**Do not check the answer key until you have answered all 10 questions.**



**Scoring:** Award 1 point for each correct answer. A score of 8/10 or higher passes.

4\. A — Never disclose verification codes in response to unexpected messages.

5\. A — Spear phishing targets a specific person or group.
